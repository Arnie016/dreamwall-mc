# DreamWall MC Competition Goal

## Goal

Win the Build Small Hackathon by making DreamWall MC feel like a new way to interact with a world: a public Minecraft canvas where prompts become plots, plots gain value through context, and nearby ideas fuse into shared artifacts.

## Submission Bet

DreamWall is not a chatbot and not a normal image generator. It is a social art market inside Minecraft:

- people submit prompts through the Hugging Face Space
- each prompt gets a plot on a flat-world canvas
- nearby concepts fuse when semantic/context rules line up
- the system assigns demo value based on density, adjacency, rarity, and votes
- the Minecraft server becomes the proof surface

## What To Avoid For V1

- Real-money payments
- Blockchain/NFT claims
- Complex ownership law
- Persistent public multiplayer economy before the demo works

Use **demo points** and **auction-style voting** first. That gives the same emotional loop without compliance risk.

## Winning Demo

One continuous video:

1. Open the Hugging Face Space.
2. Submit: `a bird in the sky`.
3. Show assigned plot, value, and fusion candidates.
4. Submit: `an AI logo for my company`.
5. Submit: `clouds gathering around the public tree`.
6. Show the system fusing nearby works into a shared concept.
7. Join the Minecraft flat world.
8. Walk to the giant canvas and show names, values, and fused plot labels.
